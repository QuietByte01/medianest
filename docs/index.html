Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Overview

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Welcome to MediaNest. Your privacy is our top priority. MediaNest is designed as a privacy-first, local-first media player and manager. We believe your personal media—your photos, videos, and music—should remain private and under your complete control.

Summary: MediaNest operates entirely on your device. We do NOT collect, upload, sell, or share your personal files, photos, videos, audio recordings, or personal data to any external server or third party.

Device Permissions & Usage

To provide advanced media playback, floating picture-in-picture, and hidden folder scanning, MediaNest requests the following device permissions:

1. Media & Storage Access (`READ_MEDIA_*` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)

  • Purpose: Used via Android MediaStore API to scan, index, organize, and play your local photos, videos, and audio files.
  • Data Handling: All file scanning and playback occurs strictly on your device using standard MediaStore APIs. No file content is ever transmitted over the internet.

2. Audio Recording / Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to render real-time audio visualizer effects and equalizer frequency spectrums during music playback.
  • Data Handling: Audio input is processed transiently in device memory only. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

3. Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`)

  • Purpose: Ensures uninterrupted background audio playback and supports floating video playback (Picture-in-Picture) when switching between apps or when the screen is turned off.

4. Bluetooth & System Notifications

  • Bluetooth (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`): Used to detect connected wireless headphones or Bluetooth audio devices for media routing.
  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Displays playback controls (play, pause, skip, album art) in the device notification shade and lock screen.

Analytics

MediaNest uses Google Firebase Analytics to gather anonymous usage statistics, crash logs, and performance metrics. This data helps us improve app stability and optimize playback across various Android devices.

  • No Personal Information: Firebase Analytics does not track your personal media files, photos, videos, or personally identifiable data.
  • User Opt-Out: You can enable or disable Analytics at any time directly in the app under Settings > Telemetry & Analytics.

Children's Privacy

MediaNest does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is a local utility designed for general audiences.
